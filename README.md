# HackUTA

Odyssey-themed hackathon project for a 3–4 person team, built on Next.js.

## Ideas

| # | Idea | What it does | Prizes |
|---|------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | **Ithaca: FaZe comeback quests** | Fans join a "crew" (squad). Every day the crew sails one leg: watch a clip, make a prediction, complete a challenge. Crews race to Ithaca for a merch drop. Brands sponsor legs to make money. | Main, FaZe, Gemini, MongoDB |
| 2 | **Homer Live: AI match commentary** | Paste a FaZe stream or clip link. Gemini reads the action, and ElevenLabs turns it into epic, Homer-style commentary ("And lo, the sniper struck from afar..."). Fans share the clips, which brings them back. | Main, FaZe, ElevenLabs, Gemini |
| 3 | **Nobody: anonymous fan identity** | Odysseus told the Cyclops his name was "Nobody." Fans start anonymous and earn their name and badges through activity. Rare badges are Solana collectibles. Pays through a premium pass. | Main, FaZe, Solana, MongoDB |
| 4 | **Penelope's Loom: shared progress board** | Penelope wove by day and unwove by night. This app tracks team or study goals and "unravels" progress you skip. A Gemini coach and a voice check-in each morning. | Main, Gemini, ElevenLabs, Tiger Data |
| 5 | **Siren Shield: scam call detector** | You FaceTime or call an AI that listens or watches. It warns you when a caller sounds like a scam ("Sirens luring you onto the rocks"). | Main, Chert, ElevenLabs, Gemini |
| 6 | **The Long Way Home: trip planner** | Describe a trip. Gemini plans a 10-stop "odyssey" with monsters (delays, costs), and an ElevenLabs narrator gives the briefing. | Main, Gemini, ElevenLabs, MongoDB |

## Picks

- **Best mix of fun, theme and prizes: #2 Homer Live.** The demo is easy and funny, it is clearly Odyssey-themed, and it can win FaZe and ElevenLabs together. It could also become a feature inside Fan Odyssey.
- **Highest prize total:** Fan Odyssey with Homer Live added as the narrator.

## Team split (4 people)

1. Frontend and game UI
2. Gemini prompts and backend
3. ElevenLabs voice and the demo
4. FaZe business pitch, slides and the DEV post
